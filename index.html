Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before confirming your trip.

Can I customize the packages?

Yes. All our packages are fully customizable. You can alter destinations, add days, switch hotel categories, or update your transport mode according to your mood and budget.

Can I book hotels through you?

Absolutely. You can either choose your own hotel or let us arrange one. If booked through us, we ensure wholesale pricing and vetted quality stays.

Do you provide free guide service?

Yes. When you book a multi-city package, we can arrange a complimentary 1-day guide in your preferred city.

Are tolls and parking included?

Local packages usually include fuel and driver allowance. Outstation trips may include tolls and parking based on actual receipts, which are shared transparently.
